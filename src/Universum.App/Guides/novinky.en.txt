# Guide – NEWS AND CONTENT
The **NEWS AND CONTENT** group shows you what is coming out: film and series **premieres**, **new episodes** of series,
your **HISTRA lists**, **posters from TMDB** and **interesting news** from the film world. From here you send a title straight to
processing, and the Dashboard also draws on your picks.

!info The sources are asked **only at the first start of the day** or with the [Refresh] button. Stored data is shown at once.

## Premieres
Four colored cards: **FILM – SK/CZ premieres**, **FILM – WORLD premieres**, **SERIES – SK/CZ premieres** and
**SERIES – WORLD premieres**. The number on a card is the number of titles. *SK/CZ* are only Czech and Slovak titles, *SVET* all the others.
1. Click a card, e.g. **FILM – SK/CZ premieres**.
2. Choose a view: [Past week], [Upcoming], [History] or [Show everything] (history, last week
  and upcoming at once).
3. Tick a title you look forward to in the **Selected** column. The [Show selected] option then shows only the selected ones.
4. In the **Notes** column you can write anything – a pasted web address turns into a link word.
- Films have the columns **Film title**, **Genre**, **Production**, **Premiere**, **When**, **Day**, **VOD / TV / cinema**.
- Series also have **Season · episode**, **Frequency** and **Episodes**.
- Next to the title are the links [TMDB] and [HISTRA]; the green line **▶ service (country)** tells where the title can be watched legally.
- [Show posters too] adds posters, the **POSTER size** slider makes them up to 2× bigger. The [Search:] field also searches without accents.
!tip A premiere moves to **History** the day after its date and stays there for good.
!pozor Czech and Slovak series need a source – add it in **Settings → News sources**.

## News – Series
Episodes of running series from the last week, on the **SERIES – CZ/SK** and **SERIES – WORLD** cards. The series are
sorted alphabetically with the next episode; the arrow on the left expands the **episode tree**.
1. Check the **Input folder** at the top – that is where UNIVERSUM looks for downloaded episodes ([Change…], [Open]).
2. Tick episodes in the tree or the box in the **Process** column (all aired episodes of the series).
3. Click [Odoslať na spracovanie]. **One file** goes to *Single processing*, **more files** (even from several
  series) to *Batch processing*.
- The **Next** column shows the next episode and a green **✓ in folder** when the file is already waiting in the input.
- **Selected** and **Notes** are shared with Premieres – a series selected here is selected there too.
- Views [Past week], [Upcoming], [History], [Show everything]; the option [only from my HISTRA lists].
- An episode that is not in the input folder yet waits below in **Waiting for the file in the input folder** –
  after downloading it, click [Check again].
!info While Batch processing is working, series wait in the **Ready for Batch processing** block –
  you send them later with the [To Batch processing] button. Files are **only read**.

## HISTRA lists
Your film and series lists from HISTRA. UNIVERSUM **only reads** them (it writes nothing there) and keeps them only on your computer.
1. Save your HISTRA token in **Settings**.
2. Click [Import lists…], tick the lists (or [Select all]) and confirm with [Importovať vybrané].
3. Click a title's poster – its **card** opens on the left: poster, links, identifiers, titles, genre, director,
  cast, plot and collection.
4. From the card go on with [Open in TMDB Poster] (choosing a poster) or [To Single processing].
- [To Single processing] finds the title in the input folder: a film or a single episode goes to *Single processing*,
  more episodes to *Batch processing*.
- [Synchronize] loads all imported lists again – otherwise they **do not refresh** by themselves.
- Change the order of the lists by **dragging the handle** or with the arrows; the bin removes a list from UNIVERSUM (it stays in HISTRA).
!tip The [Search:] field searches all lists at once – the Czech or original title, or the year.

## TMDB poster
Finds the poster of a film or series and saves it as **folder.jpg** – into the **POSTER** folder in the output or
right next to the processed title.
1. Type the title (with the year too, e.g. *Vymyslený film 2026*), a TMDB or IMDb ID, choose *Films and series*,
  *Films* or *Series* and click [Search].
2. Click a result – the title's card appears on the left, the **Poster** block on the right.
3. Choose a poster; the recommended one has a **★** and UNIVERSUM explains below it why it recommends it.
4. Set the compression: [Size of folder.jpg:] (recommended *1000 × 1500*) and [JPEG quality:] (90 = almost like the original).
5. Click [Process the poster]; you find the result via [Open the POSTER folder].
- [Merge the poster with the film/series] saves the poster into the film's (*MOVIES*) or series' (*TVSHOWS*) main folder.
  If the title is only in the input so far, the poster **waits** until UNIVERSUM processes it.
- When TMDB has no poster, [Suggest a poster with AI] helps.
### More TMDB pictures
After ticking [More TMDB images] you pick with the mouse the **background** (*fanart.jpg*), the **title logo** (*clearlogo.png*),
and for a series also **season posters** and **episode pictures**. The [Process more TMDB images] button saves them;
[Merge more TMDB images with the film/series] puts them next to the processed title.
!pozor An existing file is not overwritten – it is renamed to *folder-povodny.jpg* (or *…-povodny*).

## Interesting news
News, reviews and interesting stories from the film world – **translated into your language**; the full articles stay with their authors.
1. Add sources in **Settings → Interesting news**; save a DeepL key there for translation.
2. Choose the period [Today], [This week] or [Everything (History)].
3. Click a source's colored label to show only its articles (another click = all).
4. [Read the whole article] (or [Read]) opens the article on the author's page.
- [Original / translation] and [Original] switch one article, the [Show original] option all at once.
- The star **☆** marks a favorite article; [Favorites only ★] shows only those.
!info Without a DeepL key the articles are shown in their original language.

## Dashboard
- **This week – series** and **This week – films**: episodes of selected series and this week's premieres
  (★ = selected title). The [News – Series] and [Premieres] buttons open the full pages.
- **Where to watch – selected titles**: where the selected titles can be watched legally in Slovakia and Czechia; **NEW** = a new
  offer. [Check] checks right away (otherwise once a day).
- **Ready in the input folder**: series episodes from News and selected films that are already waiting in the input –
  [Process] sends them to processing.
![Dashboard – This week](dashboard-week.jpg)

## Tips
- Tick **Selected** – the Dashboard draws on it (*This week*, *Where to watch*, selected films in the input folder).
- Set the **Input folder** once – it applies to Premieres, News, HISTRA lists and the Dashboard.
- A title from a HISTRA list gets a poster in two clicks: [Open in TMDB Poster] → [Process the poster].
- If something is missing, try [Refresh] – otherwise the sources are asked only once a day.
