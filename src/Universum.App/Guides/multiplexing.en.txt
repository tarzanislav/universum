# Guide – MULTIPLEXING
The **MULTIPLEXING** group is for putting together and editing MKV files: you **merge** tracks from several files into one,
fix the **header** (track names, languages, flags), edit **chapters** and look at **information** about the tracks.
The originals **never change** – the result is always a **new file** (next to the source or in the library).

## Multiplexing
Here you put a film, audio and subtitles together into **one new MKV file** – you choose the tracks, their order, names, languages and flags.
### Steps
1. Click [Add files…] or **drag** the files into the window – e.g. *Vymyslený film (2026).mkv*
   and *Vymyslený film (2026).sk.srt*.
2. UNIVERSUM asks **what to do with the files** – add them to the current settings, create new settings,
   append them to an existing file (continuation) or add them as further parts. Confirm with [OK].
3. In the **Tracks in the output** list keep ticked in the **Take** column only the tracks you want.
4. Click [UNIVERSUM rules] – the track names, languages, flags and order are filled in. Check them.
5. Choose [film] or [seriál], fill in [Titul:] (or click [TMDB]), for a series also [Epizóda:] (e.g. S01E04).
6. Click [Start multiplexing] (adds to the queue and starts) or [Add to queue] (only adds).

### Source files
- Columns **File name**, **Container**, **Size**, **Folder**; each file has **its own colour**, and its tracks have
  the same colour.
- [Remove] drops the selected file with its tracks, [Clear everything] drops all – **nothing changes on the disk**.
- [Information] shows the selected file on the **Information** page.
- The **right mouse button** offers e.g. *Append files…* (part 2 of a film), *Add files as further parts…*
  (a VOB split into parts), *Set the output file name from the selected file*, *Select all tracks from the file*.
### Tracks in the output and Properties
- Columns **Take**, **Type**, **Codec**, **Language**, **Name**, **Default**, **Forced**, **More**; the rows are in the order
  they will have in the output. Change the order with the arrows (**Ctrl+↑** / **Ctrl+↓**).
- After you select a track, you can change [Názov stopy], [Jazyk] and the flags in the **Properties** part on the right:
  [Predvolená stopa (Default)], [Zapnutá (Enabled)], [Vynútené zobrazenie (Forced)].
- [Ďalšie príznaky a rozšírené vlastnosti]: SDH, audio description, commentary, delay (ms), FPS, aspect ratio, cropping,
  subtitle character encoding (usually *cp1250* for old Czech and Slovak subtitles).
- After you select a source file, you decide whether its **Chapters**, **Attachments (fonts, pictures)** and **Tags** are taken too.
- [Výstup: názov, kapitoly, prílohy] – the output's name (title), chapters from a file (XML, OGM .txt, cue) and new attachments.
!info Tracks of an **appended file** are grey and slanted – they continue the track they belong to and take its name,
  language and flags. To edit them, click [Edit the main track].
### Output file and library
- By default the output is made **next to the first source**; with a title filled in it gets a **name by the rules**.
- [Choose…] picks another folder and name, [Reset] restores the default output.
- [Do knižnice] sends the result to the library (**MOVIES** / **TVSHOWS**) – the path then cannot be edited.
  [Library…] sets which library.
### Several settings at once
- Each output file has **its own tab**; add a new one with the **+** button (*New multiplexing settings*).
- A dot **•** on a tab means the changes are **not in the queue yet**. Closing such a tab asks first.
- At the bottom left is the state of the **queue** and the progress of the running job.
!pozor If the title is uncertain, [Confirm the title] appears – until you confirm it, writing to the library does not start.
### Preview and MEGA processing
You can **look at** the result first and send only the finished, flawless one to **MEGA processing** – it is written later, at night for example.
1. At **Náhľad:** choose *úseky: začiatok, stred, koniec (3 × 2 min)* – ready in a moment – or *celý film* (the whole film).
2. Click [Make a preview]. The preview is written to **UNIVERSUM's folder of previews**, never to the output folder.
3. [Play the preview] plays it in UNIVERSUM's player – switch the sound under the picture, the subtitles at the top right. [In another player] opens it in the Windows player.
4. When everything is right, choose *do novej dávky* (a new batch) or a waiting batch and click [Send to MEGA].
- Every tab has **its own preview** – you can prepare and compare several results at once.
- If you change the settings after the preview, UNIVERSUM tells you the preview no longer matches the result.
!info A preview is deleted with its tab or after a week. You change the folder of previews in the Settings.

## Header editing
Here you fix the **track names, languages and flags** of an MKV file **without multiplexing again**. The header is changed on a copy
in the library – it takes seconds to minutes.
1. Click [Otvoriť súbor…] or drag the file in – e.g. *Vymyslený seriál S01E02.mkv*.
2. Click [UNIVERSUM rules] – names, languages, flags and order are filled in; then you can edit them by hand.
3. In the table edit the columns **Name**, **Language**, **Default**, **Enabled**, **Forced**; the **Changes** column shows
   what will change (the original value is in the tooltip).
4. At the bottom choose [film] / [seriál], fill in [Titul:] and click [Save] (or [Add to queue]).
- [Názov (title):] is the name of the whole file that players show; leaving it empty removes it.
- [Odstrániť tagy] deletes tags (names, statistics, encoding data); [Restore from a file] discards unsaved edits.
- **▲ / ▼** change the order (audio among audio, subtitles among subtitles), **Leave out** drops the track from the result.
- The **Check** part warns e.g. about several tracks with Default or forced subtitles without Default.
!info Changing the order or leaving out a track **writes a new file** – it takes minutes. Below the table you always see
  how the result will be written. [Undo] reverts the last saved change when possible.

## Chapters
Here you **add, fix, generate or shift** a file's chapters.
1. Click [Otvoriť súbor…] – the duration and the current chapters are shown.
2. Edit **Start (HH:MM:SS.mmm)**, **Name** and **Language**. A wrong time turns red.
3. Fill in [Titul:] and click [Save] – a copy with the new chapters is saved to the library.
- [Add] inserts a chapter after the selected one (a minute later), [Remove] drops it, [Sort by time] puts them in order.
- **Every** … **min** and [Generate] replace the list with chapters over the whole length of the file.
- **Shift** … **s** and [Shift all] move all chapters at once (negative too, e.g. -1).
- [Load from a file…] (XML or OGM .txt), [Remove all], [Restore from a file], [Undo].
!pozor UNIVERSUM **does not change** a file with several editions or nested chapters – edit it in MKVToolNix GUI.

## Information
Here you **look at the tracks** of any video (MKV, MP4, AVI, TS…) – nothing is changed here.
1. Click [Otvoriť súbor…] or click a file in the **Files in progress** list on the left (files from Multiplexing and Batch
   processing).
2. The container, duration and the number of tracks, chapters, tags and attachments are shown at the top.
3. The table has the columns **ID**, **Type**, **Codec**, **Language**, **Name**, **Default**, **Enabled**, **Forced**,
   **More flags** and **Properties**; the rows are **coloured by track type**.
![Information about a file from Multiplexing](info-files.jpg)

## Tips
- **First [UNIVERSUM rules], then edits by hand** – the rules can be applied again at any time.
- For tracks with an **undefined language**, choose the language and apply the rules again.
- Write [Titul:] as *Title (year)*, e.g. *Vymyslený film (2026)*; you can also paste a TMDB address.
- [Doplnok:] marks a different version of a title, e.g. *Directors Cut* or *Remaster*.
!tip Add several jobs with [Add to queue] and run them together on the **Job queue** page.
